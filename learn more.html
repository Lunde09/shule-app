Discover More About Our Technical Programs

Iyunga Tech Secondary School stands out as a center of excellence for technical education. We focus on bridging the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical applications, giving our students a head start in science and industrial arts.

Our Core Technical Specialties

  • Mechanical Engineering: Fundamentals of machinery, tools, and technical drawings.
  • Electrical Installation: Principles of electrical circuits, wiring, and domestic power systems.
  • Civil Engineering & Building Construction: Basics of architecture, brickwork, and structural integrity.
  • Computer Science & IT: Introducing coding, computing logic, and digital literacy.

Why Choose Iyunga Tech?

We believe in providing well-equipped engineering workshops, experienced technical instructors, and a community environment that challenges students to solve real-world problems creatively.
